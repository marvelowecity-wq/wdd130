Jinja White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to bring the thrill of the Nile to everyone! At Jinja White Water Rafting, our mission is to provide safe, exciting, and unforgettable adventures from Jinja, Uganda - the source of the Nile.

History

Founded in 2018 in Jinja,our company started with a small team of river guides who loved the Nile.

Today we have guided over 2,000 adventurers through rapids like The Big Mallard and Los Dientes del Diablo. Safety, fun, and the spirit of Uganda is our story.
