Jinja White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to bring the thrill of the Nile to everyone! Our mission is safe, exciting, and unforgettable white water adventures from Jinja, Uganda - the source of the Nile.

History

Founded in 2018 in Jinja, our company started with a small team of river guides who loved the Nile. Now we guide hundreds of adventurers every year through the best rapids in Uganda.
